Privacy Policy — KHSAE TEI (ខ្សែតី)

Last updated: August 10, 2026

KHSAE TEI does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to us or to any third party. The app communicates directly with a companion desktop application over your local network only — nothing leaves your WiFi network.

Permissions we ask for, and why

  • Camera — to scan the QR code the desktop app displays for pairing. Never used for anything else; no images are stored or transmitted.
  • Local network access — to discover the desktop app on your WiFi network (mDNS) and connect to it.

What we don't do

We do not use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs. We do not have a server, a database, or an account system — there is nothing for us to collect.
